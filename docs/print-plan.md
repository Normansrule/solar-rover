> Generated by `python3 tools/print_plan.py` from the print table in [assembly.md](assembly.md) and the STLs. Don't edit by hand. Grams match PrusaSlicer within 7% and hours within about 20% (generic i3-style printer; a fast CoreXY printer takes about half the time). Your slicer has the final word.
